# Exhibition & Edition Kit — by Enteleora

Wall labels, price lists, certificates of authenticity, an edition register, condition reports and consignment notes for artists.

**Use it:** https://matteocamata-cloud.github.io/enteleora-kit/

Runs in your browser. No account. Everything you type stays in your own browser on your computer; download a backup from the "Import & backup" tab. Tested in Chrome.

Certificates are the artist's own declaration, not independent authentication. Consignment notes are records, not legal contracts.
